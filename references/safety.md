# Safety and data-use boundaries

- Work with public data only.
- Do not bypass authentication, anti-bot systems, access controls or private-account settings.
- Do not collect or expose API keys, cookies, private tokens or private messages.
- Avoid unnecessary retention of personal identifiers.
- Do not infer sensitive personal traits from social posts/comments.
- Do not attempt deanonymization.
- For high-stakes domains such as medical, legal or financial topics, social content is audience/context evidence—not professional truth.
- Respect data-provider terms, platform terms and applicable law.
