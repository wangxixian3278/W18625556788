# Platform selection guide

Always confirm the current TikHub MCP platform list dynamically with:

```bash
python3 scripts/tikhub_mcp.py platforms
```

General research fit (not hard limits):

- **Douyin** — China short-video trends, creators, consumer topics, local/lifestyle content
- **Xiaohongshu** — consumer intent, lifestyle, beauty, fashion, travel, product research, recommendation language
- **TikTok** — global short-video culture, creators, trends, product discovery
- **Instagram** — visual creators, lifestyle, fashion, Reels, brand presence
- **YouTube** — long-form explanations, reviews, tutorials, creator channels and comments
- **Bilibili** — China long-form/knowledge/ACG/technology communities
- **Weibo** — public discussion, entertainment, news-adjacent trends and fan communities
- **Reddit** — question-rich community discussion, niche pain points, product comparison
- **Zhihu** — question/answer and knowledge-oriented public discussion
- **Kuaishou** — short-video creator and regional/community audiences
- **LinkedIn** — professional/B2B/public company and career content
- **WeChat** — public-account articles and content ecosystems where available
- **X/Twitter** — real-time public discussion and creator/company commentary
- **Threads** — public conversation and creator discussion

For "all platforms" requests, start with 3–5 platforms that best represent the target market and research question, then expand only if the first sample shows additional value.
