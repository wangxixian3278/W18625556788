# Research playbooks

## A. Niche map
1. Search broad seed terms.
2. Extract repeated nouns, problems, hashtags, creator categories and product terms.
3. Cluster into sub-niches.
4. Re-search high-signal sub-niches.
5. Rank by demand, momentum and supply.

## B. Competitor discovery
1. Search the niche with several seed terms.
2. Collect recurring authors and brands.
3. Fetch account/profile data where available.
4. Sample recent posts.
5. Rank by relevance, consistency, abnormal high-performance posts and audience response—not followers alone.

## C. Viral breakdown
1. Establish each account's recent baseline.
2. Identify relative outliers.
3. Compare topic, title/text, hook, duration/form, publishing time and comments.
4. Separate repeatable pattern from one-off event/news luck.

## D. Comment demand mining
1. Sample comments from several representative high-performing and normal posts.
2. Extract questions, objections, purchase concerns, tutorials requested, missing details and comparisons.
3. Cluster semantically.
4. Keep counts/coverage and representative examples.
5. Convert recurring unmet questions into research or content opportunities.

## E. Cross-platform comparison
1. Use equivalent search concepts, not necessarily literal translation only.
2. Normalize metrics within each platform.
3. Compare topic share, relative performance, recurring questions and content formats.
4. Explicitly note fields that are not comparable.
