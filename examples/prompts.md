# Example prompts

## 1. Full niche research

```text
Use my-research.
Research the last 30 days of the home-coffee niche on Douyin and Xiaohongshu.
Build a sub-niche map, find representative accounts, identify abnormal winners,
mine recurring audience questions and rank 10 underserved content opportunities.
Start with a small sample and estimate request volume before scaling.
```

## 2. Competitor map

```text
Use my-research.
Find representative creators in the compact-camera niche on Xiaohongshu.
Do not rank only by follower count. Compare recent consistency, relative post performance,
content themes and recurring audience demand.
```

## 3. Product research

```text
Use my-research.
Research public discussion of PRODUCT_NAME across YouTube, Reddit and TikTok.
Separate observed product claims from audience opinions and analytical inference.
Find common buying concerns, comparisons, missing information and content opportunities.
```

## 4. Cross-platform opportunity

```text
Use my-research.
Research the running-shoe niche in France across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube for 60 days.
Normalize within each platform. Find which questions and subtopics are strong on one platform
but still under-supplied on another.
```

## 5. Account audit

```text
Use my-research.
Audit this public creator account.
Establish a recent baseline, identify posts above 2x the account median,
cluster the topics behind those winners and explain what appears repeatable versus one-off.
```
